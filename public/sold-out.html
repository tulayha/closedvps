When Hetzner says CX23 isn't available

Since June 2026 Hetzner has often marked its cost-optimized servers, the x86 CX and the ARM CAX types, as unavailable. Its status page blames high demand and limited hardware, and on top of that it turns down new servers for new accounts and for some older ones chosen at random (a write-up of what changed). The servers come back into stock now and then during the day, and whoever asks first gets them.

What the in-stock plans cost

In September 2026 a 4 GB CX23 was 5.99 EUR a month in Germany and Finland, with IPv4 and before VAT, while the nearest plan that stayed in stock, the CPX22, was 19.99 EUR. At 8 GB the CX33 was 8.99 EUR against 35.99 EUR for the CPX32, and at 16 GB the CX43 was 16.49 EUR against 69.99 EUR. A server you already rent keeps its old price, so check before you delete or resize one.

Widen what you'll take

Stock is per type and per location, so a list of a few types across a few locations comes up sooner than one CX23 in one place. The ARM CAX11 is another 4 GB server, at 6.49 EUR, and Hetzner's European locations are Nuremberg (nbg1), Falkenstein (fsn1) and Helsinki (hel1).

Buy it the minute it's back

closedvps create --type cx23:6,cax11:7,cx33:9 --location nbg1,fsn1,hel1

ClosedVPS checks every minute and buys the first server on your list that's in stock, each type under its own monthly cap in your account's currency. A refusal that waiting can fix counts as another round, and one it can't, such as a bad token or a full account limit, stops it with Hetzner's own message. While it waits it keeps your computer from sleeping on idle, and it alerts you when it buys a server and again when the server answers SSH, on your desktop and, if you give it a link, through ntfy, Discord, Slack, Telegram or a webhook. For waits longer than a laptop stays open, run it in Docker on a machine that stays on.

What you get

The server comes up behind a Hetzner firewall with no inbound rules, reachable only over Tailscale, with SSH keys only, no root login and security updates that reboot it when needed. closedvps create --dry-run prints the whole setup before you pay for anything.
