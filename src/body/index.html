Why every port is closed

In October a Linux bug let anyone on the internet take over a server listening for Multipath TCP, and Ubuntu had no fix for two weeks after that was shown. A server behind a firewall with no inbound rules never received those packets, and its daily updates rebooted it into the fix once Ubuntu had one. How each part of ClosedVPS lines up with NIST, CISA, CIS and the vendors' own docs.

If Tailscale stops working

Open port 22 to your own IP, fix Tailscale over SSH, then close it again.

$ closedvps open-ssh vps
$ ssh dev@203.0.113.1
$ closedvps close-ssh vps
